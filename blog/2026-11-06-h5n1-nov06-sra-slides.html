Slide 1 of 4 · Big Picture

Half of the 14 Overnight Samples Are Outside Washington

  • Overnight on November 6, 2026, USDA added 14 new raw H5N1 sequencing runs (SRR41562233–SRR41562246), bringing the Fall 2026 dataset to 42 cow genomes across 22 dairy herds.
  • Placing all 42 sequences on the H5N1 family tree shows they come from three separate regional outbreaks:
    • Washington (+6 new): 29 cows across 12 herds (+ 2 human cases).
    • Texas (+6 new): 9 cows across 7 herds.
    • Idaho (+2 new): 4 cows across 3 herds.
  • Cows inside Washington differ by just 1.1 mutations on average (~3–4 weeks of local spread), whereas Washington, Idaho, and Texas viruses are 61 to 67 mutations apart (~1.5 years of separate evolution).
Slide 2 of 4 · Washington Outbreak

Inside Washington: 12 Herds from One Recent Jump

  • One shared founder (~3–4 weeks before Oct sampling): All 12 Washington herds (29 cows + 2 humans) share an identical 10-mutation stem and trace back to a single introduction seeded by just 1 to 3 cows.
  • Main multi-farm branch doubled (2 → 4 herds): Two new November 6 herds (26G15051 and 26G15055) carry the exact same two quiet genetic tags (PA:G1260A + MP:T462C) seen in two October herds.
  • Tank-to-tank split inside a single farm (26G14833): Tank 1 carries PB1:H99Y (83%), while Tanks 2 & 3 carry M2:S82N (45–57%) + PA:Y290H (37–73%)—tracing back to a 20% minor variant in an October herd (26G14594).
Slide 3 of 4 · Idaho & Texas

Active Farm-to-Farm Spread in Idaho and Texas

Idaho Mammalian-Polymerase Branch (NODE_0002210)

  • Carries two polymerase mutations: PB2:T271A (a known mammalian adaptation) paired with PB2:D678G.
  • New November 6 sample 26G14741-001 is 0 consensus mutations away from October 30 sample 26G14109-002.
  • Confirms active herd-to-herd transmission of this mammalian-adapted branch across 3 sampled batches (4 cows).

Texas 5-Mutation Sweep Branch (NODE_0002254)

  • 6 of the 14 overnight runs belong to the Texas lineage (expanding it from 3 to 9 cows across 7 herds).
  • Three earlier samples (26G09480-002/-004/-008) sit right at the root of the Texas branch.
  • Three newer samples (26G13259, 26G15081, 26G15291) carry a 5-amino-acid sweep across five viral genes (PB1, PA, HA, NP, NA).
Slide 4 of 4 · Brief Technical Note

2024 "Rescue Primers" Create Fake Mutations Unless Trimmed

  • Why 6 samples look unusual at gene tips: 6 weak-RNA samples were amplified with a 32-primer rescue kit (-original-tile) designed from 2024 H5N1 sequences.
  • Where the 2026 virus has evolved away from 2024, those synthetic primer ends overwrite the real 2026 virus at the ends of sequencing reads.
  • Trimming 25 bp from read ends fixes the problem: Apparent mutations like NA:I5V (95% → 0%) and PA:K716E (100% → 0%) vanish completely once primer ends are trimmed off.
