Why 6 New Genomes Appeared to Share Fake Mutations

  • Two Lab Protocols in One Release: 8 runs used standard whole-segment RTPCR (-original: 0 internal dropouts), while 6 runs used rescue tiled amplicons (-original-tile: sharp troughs at PB2:406–685, PB1:26–194, NA:1–14).
  • 2024 Primers Overwrite 2026 Viruses: At 14 positions where 2025–2026 cattle H5N1 evolved away from the 2024 sequence, un-trimmed 2024 tile primers stamp ancestral bases onto read ends.
  • False NA:I5V in 6/6 Tiled Runs: Immediately after the NA:1–9 dropout, a 2024 G-primer forces 91–99% NA:A15G (95% → 0% in inner reads ≥25 bp from read ends).
  • False Internal Mixtures: Tile boundaries also create synthetic M1:N82S (34% → 1%), PA:A894R (37% → 2%), and NP:G119R (17% → 2%).
⚠️ Surveillance Rule: Always scrub 2024 tile-primer binding sites in -original-tile SRA runs before building trees or calling amino-acid substitutions.

Inside Washington: A 4-Herd Subclade & Live Bulk-Tank Splitting

  • PA:G1260A + MP:T462C Doubles to 4 Herds: New Nov 6 batches 26G15051-001 and 26G15055-001 carry the exact 2-SNP barcode seen on Oct 30 in 26G14501 and 26G14598; 26G15055-001 adds a 64% PB1:C1275T + NP:V182I sub-mix.
  • Within-Herd Divergence in 26G14833: Tank -001 fixes PB1:H99Y (83%), while tanks -002 and -003 co-segregate PA:Y290H (37% → 73%) and M2:S82N (45% → 57%).
  • Cross-Herd Bridge to Oct 30: That same M2:S82N variant was already present at 20% in Oct 30 batch 26G14594-002.
  • Human vs. Cattle Selection: Oct 29 human isolate UW-110701 carries within-host HA receptor-binding variants (H125N 18%, Q226R 16%, S181P 11%) that are 0% across all 29 cattle samples.
