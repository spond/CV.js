Why did a pediatric respiratory virus suddenly shift to adults across 13 European countries?

Enterovirus D68 (EV-D68) infects the upper airway, causing severe childhood wheezing and—in rare clusters—polio-like spinal cord paralysis (acute flaccid myelitis, AFM). In autumn 2025 (Dyrdak et al., 2026), European surveillance documented a dramatic shift:

88% A2/D
Continent-Wide Takeover

Subclade A2/D (lineage A2/D-25d) displaced B3 across 13 European countries after a decade of B3 dominance.

39 vs. 4 Yrs
+35-Year Adult Age Shift

Median patient age jumped from 4 years in subclade B3 to 39 years in subclade A2/D.

194 WGS
Beyond VP1 (13% of Genome)

Analyzing all 1,571 VP1 sequences and 194 complete genomes with ChronAeon, HyphAeon, and RhizAeon reveals how the wave emerged.

Wastewater Early Radar

VP2 + VP3 + VP1
Pre-2025 Capsid Remodeling

Antigenic escape in adults was built before 2025 across 6 VP1 trunk codons plus fixed substitutions in the VP2 EF-loop and VP3.
