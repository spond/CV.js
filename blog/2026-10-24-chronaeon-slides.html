Slide 2 of 6 · How the ChronAeon Stack Works

Profile rooting, foundation covariance, AutoClock, and online coresets

1. Profile Root ($\mathbf{Q}$)
Continuous Ancestral Anchor

Weights sequences exponentially toward earlier sampling dates to form a soft nucleotide profile $\mathbf{Q} \in \Delta^{L \times 4}$, contracting saturated distances back into the linear regime.

2. HyphAeon PGLS
Attention Covariance & Fieller CIs

Extracts cross-taxa attention covariance $\mathbf{K}$ from HyphAeon in one forward pass and computes exact asymmetric confidence sets via Fieller's theorem ($g < 1$).

3. AutoClock
Spectral Laplacian Partitioning

Recursively bisects the normalized graph Laplacian along its Fiedler vector to separate co-circulating rate regimes while flagging high-leverage outliers via Sherman-Morrison LOOCV.

4. Online ALD Coreset
Million-Taxon Streaming ($N \gg L$)

Locks 256 ancestral anchors + 1,792 online Approximate Linear Dependence (ALD) landmarks to stream millions of genomes in $\mathcal{O}(N \cdot M)$ time and flat memory.
